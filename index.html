Why HNP exists

A party formed to return politics to the people.

India’s political scenario feels mixed and noisy. Too often, citizens see leaders working for personal benefit instead of public benefit. Holiness National Party was formed to change that habit of politics.

HNP believes democracy should not be a word people hear only during elections. It should be a daily experience: leaders listening, decisions being explained, public money being respected, and ordinary people feeling seen.

Present demands

Issues HNP wants India to act on now.

Fuel choice

Free petrol from compulsory ethanol blending.

Regular petrol and E20 should both remain visibly available, with clear pump labels and fair pricing.

Publish independent vehicle-impact studies, track mileage complaints, repair costs, warranty disputes, and protect owners of older vehicles.

Flood-free India

Stop treating floods as a yearly routine.

Assam, Gujarat, and many other states face recurring devastation. HNP demands prevention before relief.

Upgrade drainage, embankments, river monitoring, early warnings, wetland protection, hill-area regulation, rescue capacity, and fast rehabilitation for affected families.

Youth work

Create practical jobs and skill pathways.

Young people need employable skills, local enterprise support, and transparent hiring systems.

Set up district skill hubs, apprenticeship guarantees, startup help desks, and public dashboards for vacancies and recruitment timelines.

Clean services

Make government work without harassment.

Citizens should not need middlemen, repeated visits, or bribes for basic public services.

Time-bound service delivery, receipt-based tracking, grievance escalation, public officer ratings, and strict action on corruption.

Education

Protect students from broken systems.

Exams, admissions, and learning must be reliable, affordable, and useful for real life.

Transparent exam calendars, leak-proof testing, mental-health support, vocational learning, and stronger public-school infrastructure.

Health access

Bring basic healthcare closer to people.

Every family deserves reachable clinics, emergency care, affordable medicines, and clean drinking water.

Strengthen primary health centres, ambulance response, generic medicine access, water testing, and district-level health accountability.
